Cat Travel Arena Publisher

An internal, single-owner publishing tool for short cat comedy videos.

Purpose of this application

Cat Travel Arena Publisher is a private automation tool built and operated by one person for their own content project, "พี่เสือ & น้องยูสุ" (Suea & Yuzu) — a series of short, vertical, comedy travel videos featuring two cats.

The tool takes a video that its owner has already created and edited, and uploads it to that same owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram accounts so the episode can be published across platforms at a scheduled time. It exists to automate a repetitive manual upload task for a single creator — nothing more.

What this application does

  • Uploads video files and their captions that the account owner has already reviewed and approved.
  • Connects to TikTok, YouTube (Google), Facebook, and Instagram using OAuth access that the owner personally grants, solely to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts.
  • Hosts an approved video file temporarily on a private object-storage bucket so that Instagram's publishing API can fetch it during the upload.

What this application does not do

  • It is not a public service, SaaS product, or multi-tenant application. There is no sign-up and no third-party user base.
  • It does not collect data from viewers, followers, or any other platform user.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer data to third parties, and does not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • It does not upload, host, or republish content belonging to anyone other than its owner.

Who can use it

Only the account owner. There is no public login and no account creation; the application is run locally by its owner against their own credentials.
