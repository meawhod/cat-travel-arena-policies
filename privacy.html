Privacy Policy

Cat Travel Arena Publisher is a single-owner internal tool. It does not operate as a public service and does not knowingly collect personal data from any third party.

Data this tool accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram accounts, used solely to upload the owner's own video content to those same accounts.
  • Video files, captions, and hashtags prepared by the account owner for publishing.

Data this tool does not do

  • It does not collect data from viewers, followers, or any other platform user.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use collected data for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens and video files are stored only on infrastructure controlled by the account owner (local storage and a private Cloudflare R2 bucket used solely to give Instagram's API a fetchable video URL during publishing). Videos hosted there are temporary and not intended for public discovery.

Third-party platforms

This tool only interacts with TikTok, YouTube (Google), and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they separately handle data once content is uploaded.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner via the associated social media accounts (e.g. TikTok/Instagram: @phiseua.yuzu).
